About

A community built on a simple idea.

Bring together phenomenal women working at the frontier — foster connections, and explore new frontiers of AI, together.

Our story

Women in Frontier AI started as a community group — created from a deep desire to bring together phenomenal women working at the frontier of AI. Not a company, not a conference series; a group of people who wanted to know each other.

The women building the next generation of AI systems are often scattered — across labs, companies, countries, and subfields. We exist to close that distance: to foster real connections, and to give people a place to understand and explore new frontiers of AI alongside people doing the same work.

We welcome women working at the frontier, and the allies who support them.
